Snorlax - The Sleepy Pokémon

Welcome to your guide on everyone's favorite nap-loving giant!

Trainer Tip 💡

Snorlax might be lazy, but it's got sky-high HP and can tank hits like a champ! Don't underestimate it in battle.

Pokédex Entry

Name: Snorlax

Type: Normal

Height: 6'11" (2.11m)

Weight: 1014.1 lbs (460 kg)

Ability: Immunity / Thick Fat

Description: Snorlax is a Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation I. It is known for its huge size, massive appetite, and of course... sleeping everywhere!

Fun Facts

  • Pokédex #: 143
  • Sleeps 20 hours a day!
  • Evolves from Munchlax with high friendship.
  • Can block paths until woken with a Poké Flute!
